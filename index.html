The Problem

Applying to university
in Uzbekistan is broken.

Every university in Uzbekistan has its own application portal, its own process, its own document requirements. Students spend weeks repeating the same information across dozens of separate systems.

International students face the added challenge of navigating unfamiliar processes with little guidance. The system is fragmented, opaque, and exhausting — and it shouldn't be.

We're fixing that.

Fragmented portals

Each university runs a completely separate application system with different logins, formats, and requirements.

Repeated documentation

Students upload the same transcripts, essays, and personal details 5, 10, even 20 separate times.

No status visibility

After submitting, students hear nothing. No tracking, no timeline, no transparency.

Hard for international students

No centralized information makes it nearly impossible to navigate the landscape.
